Used cars are like a box of chocolates. The driver's door of my car lacked a check mechanism. The door check is a device with detents that holds the door open. The previous owner of my E30 had somehow torn the mechanism from the door itself. This led to a couple of problems:
1. The door wouldn't stay open if parked on any kind of slope.
2. The window would occasionally fail to roll down.

I was never in a hurry to fix the first issue, but the second was a source of frustration. The check arm would rotate freely because it was no longer attached to the door. It would, at the least convenient moment, swing underneath the window, blocking it from opening. That can be a show-stopper, especially in a car that lacks working air conditioning.

> Warning: this is a functional fix, not a cosmetic one. This car will get painted at some point in the future, so I didn't make any effort to perfect the body on this repair.

This was a fun repair. There were a couple of layers of sheet metal to account for as well as the mechanism itself.
